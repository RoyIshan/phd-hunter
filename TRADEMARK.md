# Name and artwork policy

The code is MIT licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)): you may use, copy, modify and
redistribute it, provided the licence and copyright notice stay with it.

The **name "phd-hunter"**, and the README artwork in `docs/assets/` that carries it,
are not covered by that permission. They identify this project. This policy says
what you may and may not do with them. It is a plain-language policy, not a
registered trademark.

## You may

- **Fork the repository** and contribute back through pull requests.
- **Say that you use it**, or that your project is *based on* or *a fork of*
  phd-hunter, with a link to <https://github.com/RoyIshan/phd-hunter>.
- **Redistribute it unmodified**, under its own name, with the licence and notice intact.
- **Write about it**, review it and teach with it.
- **Keep the name on a fork** while it stays a working fork you intend to merge
  back or keep in step with this project.

## You may not

- **Publish a modified or renamed copy under the name phd-hunter**, or under a name
  or logo easily mistaken for it, in a way that suggests it is this project or is
  endorsed by it.
- **Remove or replace the copyright notice** in `LICENSE`, the credit in the README,
  or `CITATION.cff` when you redistribute the code. A renamed copy must still say
  where it came from.
- **Sell or promote a product** under the name phd-hunter without written permission.

## If you build something different

Give it its own name and keep this line in its README:

> Based on [phd-hunter](https://github.com/RoyIshan/phd-hunter) by RoyIshan and contributors (MIT).

## Questions and reports

Open an issue on the repository. Misuse of the name can be reported to the
platform it appears on.
