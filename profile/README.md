# profile/

Your details live here and stay on your machine. Everything in this folder
except this file is ignored by git.

| File | What | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `profile.yml` | Facts, eligibility, targets, research themes | `/phd-hunter setup` or copy `templates/profile.example.yml` |
| `cv.md` | Master academic CV: the only source of facts for anything drafted | same, from `templates/cv.example.md` |
| `voice.md` | How you write, taken from your own letters | same, from `templates/voice.example.md` |

Fields marked `CONFIRM` are things your documents did not state. Fill them in:
eligibility verdicts depend on them.
